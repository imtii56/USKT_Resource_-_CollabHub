Our Story

StudyHub was born out of our own struggles as computer science students. We found ourselves overwhelmed with the amount of material to cover and frustrated by the lack of organized resources. That's when we decided to create a solution that would not only help us but also benefit fellow students.

Our Mission

We believe that education should be accessible, organized, and stress-free. Our mission is to:

  • Provide structured learning paths for complex subjects
  • Curate high-quality resources in one accessible place
  • Help students track their learning progress
  • Create a community of learners supporting each other

By sharing our own study materials and methods, we hope to make the learning journey easier for students who come after us.

Our Learning Journey

Here's the study path we followed that inspired the creation of StudyHub. We're sharing our curriculum to help you see what you'll learn in the coming semesters.

Year 1: Foundations

Introduction to Programming, Discrete Mathematics, Computer Fundamentals, Calculus, and Communication Skills.

Year 2: Core Concepts

Data Structures & Algorithms, Database Systems, Operating Systems, Computer Networks, and Probability & Statistics.

Year 3: Specialization

Machine Learning, Web Development, Software Engineering, Elective Courses, and Research Methodology.

Year 4: Advanced Topics

Cloud Computing, Big Data, Cybersecurity, Capstone Project, and Professional Internship.
